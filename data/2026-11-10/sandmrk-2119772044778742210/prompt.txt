The horn of the Elven kin has sounded.
The golden-haired princess turns, enters the palace, and dons her armor;
the archers draw their longbows full, curved like crescent moons;
the cry of victory pierces the very heavens.
The palace gates swing wide — the Elven host marches forth in ordered ranks,
and beneath the reviewing gaze of the High Elven Queen,
they ride forth to claim the epic that shall be theirs!
